# Design Brief: Harbor Café website (demo)

A one-page website for a neighborhood coffee shop: menu, opening hours, and pickup pre-orders.

Platform: both
Audience: Locals ordering from their phone on the way in

## How to use this brief
- Items marked **RULE** must be followed exactly.
- Items marked **INSPIRATION** show the intended feel. Adapt them; don't copy them pixel for pixel.
- Items marked **AVOID** must not appear in the build.
- Reference images are in `references/<basket>/`. Open and look at each image referenced below before building the matching part of the UI.
- If a rule conflicts with an inspiration item, the rule wins.

## Overall Direction

- RULE: Warm & organic: earth tones, soft corners, hand-drawn touches
- INSPIRATION: Feels like a chalkboard menu in a small shop, not a chain
- AVOID: Glossy gradients or stock photos of latte art

## UX Writing

- RULE: Buttons start with a verb: "Order ahead", "See menu"

## Avoid list

- AVOID: Glossy gradients or stock photos of latte art _(Overall Direction)_

## Open questions

- No references for Shapes & Surfaces — use consistent corner radii and subtle shadows.
- No references for Icons & Graphics — use a single consistent icon set (e.g. Lucide) at one stroke weight.
- No references for Navigation — use a simple top navigation that collapses on mobile.
